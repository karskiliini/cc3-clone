CC3 Clone — In-Battle UI Preview

Left-click a row in TEAMS to select. Wheel/arrows scroll the team list. Right-click on a selected team's row (or anywhere in the map area above) opens the command menu — click a row or press its hotkey (M, F, S, I, K, D, A) to pick an order, Esc/outside-click cancels. Hold Shift and move the mouse over the map area to test the LOS tool. Click PAUSE/OPTIONS/ OVERVIEW/TRUCE in the message panel to see button feedback in the log.
