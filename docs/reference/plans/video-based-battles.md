# Video-based standalone battles

078 — first scenario brief. Hostomel selected on 2026-11-04. Not yet playable.

Use public footage from past engagements as reference for standalone scenarios.
Start with one engagement and one small map. A useful candidate is the airport
entrance and nearby service area at Hostomel on 24 February 2022. The entire
airport and the full battle are beyond the pilot's scope.

Sources to review frame by frame before drawing the map:

- [Hostomel Trap, retrospective documentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rfazOWa9rSY).
- [Airborne operation Hostomel Airport, interview-based reconstruction](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bniPj92YVhU).
  The publisher explicitly describes its reconstruction as incomplete.
- [CNN's account of its footage verification and reporting](https://transcripts.cnn.com/show/cnr/date/2022-03-01/segment/22).
- [Contemporaneous account from the airport gate, PBS](https://pbswisconsin.org/watch/amanpour-co/correspondent-matthew-chance-encounters-russian-troops-komvi/).

Log each usable frame by source, timestamp and visible landmark. Align visible
roads, buildings and open ground against dated cartography; use the historical
layout rather than assuming present-day geometry is unchanged. Record terrain,
units and timing as confirmed, inferred or designed for play. Create original
game assets. Keep the video links in the briefing rather than embedding footage.

Pilot design: an infantry-scale engagement with two playable sides, a gate and
service yard as objectives, and a time limit. The briefing describes the
historical situation; player actions determine the result. Proposed objectives,
unit counts and reinforcement schedules are design choices until sourced.
Reuse the current infantry simulation for the graybox, label any proxy assets
and put period equipment in a separate scenario roster before calling the
result a reconstruction. Aircraft and helicopters can remain outside the first
playable sector. Acceptance: a reproducible source-to-map ledger and a standalone
scenario that both sides can start and finish.
