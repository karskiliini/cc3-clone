## Exact user request

> jokaisen yksikön pitää osata toimia aidosti kuin oikea ihminen toimisi. Pelin logiikan ei pidä toimia sen perusteella että mikä pelin kokonaiskuva on (tai se mitä pelaaja näkee). Jokainen yksilö pelissä pitää toimia sen mukaan mitä hän näkee, mikä hänen mielenlaatunsa on, mikä on hänen käskynsä, ja kyky toimia. Mallinna henkilöille näkö ja kuulo, jopa kipu pitää olla osatekijä. Hengästyminen, shell shock, voimien loppuminen, hikoilu, tärinä, ne kaikki vaikuttavat toimintaan, nopeuteen, tähtäystarkkuuteen, päätöksentekokykyyn.

Interpretation: every person's decisions depend on their own observations, received reports/orders, personality, mental state and actual capabilities. They must not silently inherit world truth, another person's perception, or the player's display. Shared information remains possible through explicit communication. Physiology is a gameplay abstraction; numeric thresholds and time constants require explicit calibration, not medical claims.

## Concrete knowledge leaks and seams

1. **Hidden-neighbor count leaks true enemy population.** `spotting.ts:349-358` counts every nearby enemy within four tiles after one enemy is spotted, without testing whether each neighbor was seen. That count enters the best spotter's belief. It can affect perceived strength and later corpse-based clearance. Count only independently observed contacts or explicitly uncertain estimates.
2. **Side spotting substitutes for personal acquisition in combat.** `combat.ts:231-242,376` gathers every side-spotted entity; `combat.ts:286-290` attack targeting filters by the same set, then own geometric LOS. LOS is not the soldier's successful observation: personal pain, attention, concealment and acquisition can be bypassed by another observer's sighting. A received report should orient search or authorize area fire; it must not automatically become precise live-target sight.
3. **Remote tank sight changes an unaware individual's fear.** `mind.ts:535-541,569` tests any side-spotted tank/SPG without personal distance or sight; `morale.ts:77-85,118-124` tank scare uses side sightings (within 100 m), still without personal detection/receipt. `morale.ts:65-72,133` surrender proximity also uses side sight. Replace those inputs with personal contacts/beliefs.
4. **Panic fallback reacts to another observer's exact contact.** `movement.ts:245-258` selects the nearest side-spotted enemy for flee direction. It should use the frightened soldier's own threat bearing/belief; absence of knowledge must not be filled from the side's truth.
5. **Incoming-fire source can become exact through side sight or perfect bearing.** `mind.ts:330-340` grants true shooter position if anyone on the side spotted the shooter; otherwise geometric LOS grants the exact true bearing with a guessed 60 m range. The existing latter branch does avoid exact range, but has no uncertainty in bearing. Acoustic/near-miss evidence should carry an estimated sector and confidence. Source IDs may remain inside physical event resolution but must not be exposed as identified perceived contacts without justification.
6. **Belief maintenance combines personal geometry and side intelligence.** `mind.ts:200-217` checks side-wide current enemies when deciding whether the soldier sees an empty area; corpse counts use LOS but not normal range/attention/pain limits (`188-196`). Unseen remote discoveries must not keep a belief fresh or clear it.
7. **Communication exists but is simplified.** `mind.ts:227-239` shares reduced-confidence beliefs over 10 m, or 30 m from a leader, preserving source observation time. Keep this useful age behavior. Add delivery eligibility, interruption, travel/delivery cadence, source/provenance and a bounded error model; do not let same-tick iteration cause arbitrary multi-hop propagation. Being nearby must not bypass unconsciousness or an explicitly blocked communication channel.
8. **Casualty morale is team telepathy.** `morale.ts:153-165` detects health change globally and `91-95` immediately penalizes every living teammate regardless of distance, awareness or communication. Leadership loss reads also need scrutiny. Physical injury is global truth; learning about another's injury is a perceptual/report event.
9. **AI side memory is a separate level, not an individual mind.** `ai.ts:865-886` builds side contacts from side sight. This can be a command abstraction only if information is delivered through an explicit reporting policy. More concretely, `ai.ts:888-892` removes a remembered enemy when its true `outOfAction` flag changes even without a fresh sighting. That leaks hidden destruction. A stale contact should expire or be disproved by observations, not by hidden status.
10. **Mortar observer selection implicitly connects the whole side.** `combat.ts:1573-1600` selects the best eligible friendly observer anywhere within the observation rules. There is no delivery channel check in that function. Its callers/fire-mission timing must be fully traced before deciding whether existing mechanics already supply part of that channel. Preserve lawful indirect fire from reports/orders while removing automatic perfect spotting feedback.
11. **Vehicle vision has specific bypass risks.** `spotting.ts:380-390` grants a vehicle contact from proximity to fire/damage without an eye arc/LOS check in that branch, and from seeing nearby fire rather than the hidden target in another branch. The normal vehicle branch only checks positive observer visibility, not the pain/daze factors used for soldier detection. Some of this can validly create a coarse sound/light cue, but not automatically an exact identified target. Individual crew perception and internal crew communication need inclusion, not exemption under a commander-shared mind.

Global reads are **not inherently bugs**: projectile collision, blast exposure, physical sound production, terrain, nearby bodies and damage resolution need the actual world (`combat.ts:621-689` is an example blast path). The boundary is the conversion of those events into what a person can perceive and subsequently decide. Do not hide physical targets from bullets because the shooter has not identified them.

Likewise, side/team summaries and UI visibility are different from personal knowledge. `battle.ts:302-309` updates spotting then minds/combat/morale; `battle.ts:323` runs AI by controller side. `ui/screens/battle.ts:748,840` consumes a viewer/selection visibility overlay. No new direct player-view leak was proved by this focused audit. Side-wide knowledge leaks are already enough to violate the new requirement; they must not be inaccurately described as proof that all sim behavior depends on the player's camera.

## Proposed staged scope for 056

**056A — personal perception and communication.** Define a canonical per-person observation/contact interface containing observation time, modality, confidence, source, perceived location/sector and uncertainty. Keep identification separate from presence. Maintain direct visual acquisition per observer, independent of which other friendly observer has the best score. Reports copy what the sender knew at the report time, not live entity references. Acoustic perception receives bounded noisy evidence from physical sound events; obstacles/ambient noise and hearing impairment affect delivery/quality under documented gameplay rules. Keep the current EnemyBelief concept and age decay, extend it as needed rather than duplicate it.

Route autonomous targeting, threat appraisal, flee/cover, surrender, belief clearing, casualty awareness and team/commander AI through those interfaces. Enumerate every decision consumer of `spotted*`, live enemy positions and `outOfAction` before declaring this phase complete. Explicit player orders carry intended target/area and previously conveyed knowledge, not unlimited future tracking. Indirect fire and vehicle crews receive explicit communications appropriate to their roles. Derive side/UI summaries after perception/reporting; rendering may read them but cannot write knowledge or consume simulation RNG.

**056B — one integrated condition/capability model.** Reuse fatigue, health, mind stress, pain-bout timing, daze and recovery. Add short-term breathlessness and longer-term exertion only where existing fatigue cannot express recovery behavior. Represent sweat and tremor as measurable gameplay effects (derived if possible), with their causal rules and numeric calibration documented. Use a shared capability evaluator for movement pace, sensing, weapon settling/dispersion, crew work and decision/obedience latency; preserve each individual's actual ammo, wounds, posture and role constraints. Avoid multiplying the same cause several times unintentionally. Add read-only debug reasons so observed behavior can be explained from that individual's evidence and condition.

Neither phase alone completes the entire user request. Existing queue priority stands; this is a proposed scope, not authorization to interrupt P2 or implement 056 immediately.

## Deterministic state and replay requirements

`stateHash.ts:62-80` hashes only a subset of Soldier state: it omits fatigue, beliefs, fear/motivation, daze/recovery clocks and numerous decision timers. `mind.ts:84-107` also stores future-affecting first-fire, recovery, sharing and role timers in a WeakMap. Deterministic replay can reproduce private state when fully reconstructed from the start, but current hash coverage cannot detect all divergence when it first occurs. This is a coverage gap, not proof that existing replay is broken.

Make all newly introduced future-affecting perception, queued reports, condition and decision timers explicit canonical state, or supply a side-effect-free canonical projection for hashing and reconstruction. Include applicable existing human-state omissions in that inventory. Stable entity/event iteration and seeded simulation randomness are required; UI queries/debug overlays must not draw RNG. Noise uncertainty must be sampled deterministically per event/receiver, and the same observation must not become more accurate merely because a UI asks again. Rebuild/replay must reconstruct exactly the same communication queue and timer state. Version changed replay semantics deliberately.

## Observable, falsifiable completion criteria

1. **Unseen relocation invariance:** paired fixtures with equal observer inputs and personal history, but different positions/counts/status of hidden enemies outside sensing/physical influence, yield equal audited observer decisions, beliefs and condition until a legitimate event arrives. Full world hashes need not match because the worlds intentionally differ. Include a hidden neighbor beside a visible enemy and hidden destruction of a remembered unit. Use controlled/keyed random streams so unrelated global RNG draws do not confound the assertion.
2. **Two people, different knowledge:** A sees a contact; isolated B cannot see/hear it and receives no report. A may acquire/fire; B does not get exact targeting, tank fear or a flee direction from it. A and B can each acquire the same enemy independently; selecting only a best spotter is forbidden. B's later report is delayed/imprecise and does not magically grant direct sight.
3. **Hearing without identification:** an occluded shot/engine creates an uncertain bearing/region when audible, no confirmed hidden entity ID or true exact location. Moving the source within an observationally equivalent sector must not cause perfect tracking. Masked/ineligible listeners receive no contact; observations can be wrong under the declared model.
4. **Report provenance and staleness:** disconnect the communication channel and no report arrives; restore it and only appropriate queued/current reports arrive. Old reports retain their original observation age, cannot refresh themselves in a loop, and never follow a hidden live target through a stored reference. Corpse/empty-area belief clearance requires the observer's own evidence or a delivered report.
5. **Same orders, different capability:** equal weapon/order/situation with controlled condition differences produces a lower legal pace and slower/worse aim for a winded/exhausted, wounded or trembling soldier within the calibrated design. Personality/stress alter decision latency or obedience in reproducible fixtures. Nothing permits firing, loading or moving beyond actual ability merely because an order requests it.
6. **Recovery:** after exertion and incoming stimuli stop, breathlessness/exhaustion/acute shock and corresponding penalties recover along documented bounded timelines; severe inability persists until its appropriate gate clears. New stress interrupts recovery predictably. Existing pain bouts and mental-state recovery regressions remain covered. Avoid asserting that a wound itself heals during one battle.
7. **Viewer invariance:** with identical resolved controllers, seed and command log, changing viewer side, selected soldier/team, camera, overlays and supported sprite/3D presentation produces identical simulation hashes and per-person decisions every tick. Controller defaults derived from `playerSide` must be pinned in this fixture so the test changes presentation only.
8. **Hash/replay completeness:** perturb one belief's uncertainty/age, a report delivery timer, fatigue/breathlessness, tremor-derived source state, or a daze/recovery timer and the immediate hash must change when that field can affect future behavior. Replay and two peers reconstruct identical per-tick state/hash chains, including after recovery/resync; perform the project's normal cross-engine determinism checks when implementation reaches verification.
9. **Explainability and regression:** inspect a selected individual's last decision with its observation/report, age/confidence, order and capability reason. Debug inspection leaves hash/RNG unchanged. Physical unseen hits, suppression at a believed area and legitimate communicated indirect fire still work.

Audit limit: focused code paths were inspected, not every decision caller. `vehicle.ts`, cover-seeking callers, detailed crew communication, full order tracking, hasty-fire paths, all private caches and all UI consumers require a complete implementation-time consumer inventory. There is no whole-project no-omniscience claim and no passing verification claim in this memo.
