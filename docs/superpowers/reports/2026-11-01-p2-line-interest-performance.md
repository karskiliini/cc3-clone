# P2 line-contact eligibility optimization — 2026-11-01

Baseline: `a023795`. Heavy work ran on the user's Mac mini (M2 Pro, Node 24.22.1), in `~/work/cc3-p2-perf-20261101w.noindex`. Capacity was checked first; unrelated services were preserved. Measurements from different machines are not compared.

The warmed production profile identified repeated line-contact eligibility queries as a significant cost. The per-completion query now memoizes exact region proofs: no crushable line source within the conservative 5×5 tile neighborhood means ineligible; a crushable source in every possible sample tile within the inner 3×3 means eligible. Mixed regions retain the original four interpolation weights and source checks. Field replacement or revision invalidates both caches. No forces, substeps, accepted contacts, terrain ownership or state hashes changed.

The final same-process alternating comparison used 40 moving vehicles, 1,000 warm-up ticks and ten alternating pairs of 1,000 timed ticks. Median production cost changed from **2.89562 to 2.59450 ms/tick (10.40% faster)**. Every pair had identical state hashes. This is the existing flat-awake production fixture, not a general claim for every battle or browser. The 0.5 ms P2 target remains open.

An eligibility-only diagnostic also checked empty, sparse-fence and dense-fence regions. Their median query costs improved by 61.97%, 44.03% and 31.50%, respectively. These are diagnostic query timings, not production-frame improvements. The initial negative-only candidate regressed dense-fence query cost by 17.75%; it was superseded by the positive-region proof and its timings are excluded from the final production result.

Validation: 348 tests across 19 physics, height-field, transport and towing test files passed, including an independent weighted-source eligibility oracle over sparse, empty and dense maps, boundaries, refreshes and field rebuilds. Physics-tool TypeScript and production build passed. Three scenes × 600 ticks × four vehicles produced 7,200 bit-exact body records, plus identical per-tick state hashes before/after and on the local M5 Pro / Node 24.22.1.
