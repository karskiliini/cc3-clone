# Organic mortar transport and light-gun towing — 2026-11-01

Implemented on `codex/vehicles-3d-p2`, baseline `8e73810`. The earlier precise-route feature remains reverted; movement uses the existing orders and waypoints.

Mortars get separate assigned one-driver carrier teams, with enough real passenger seats for the full crew: German Kettenkrad + Kübelwagen; Soviet two Jeeps. A distinct procedural Jeep is included. Assignment is separate from current boarding state and follows mortar deployment without taking campaign roster UIDs. Move Fast coordinates normal packing tasks, equipment approach/loading, individual boarding, vehicle routes, stopping, unloading and normal emplacement. Ordinary Move/Sneak remain ground orders. Retargeting and cancellation preserve the pending order; ground continuation explicitly avoids accidental reboarding.

A gun crew's Move Fast order chooses a compatible free nearby tractor. The existing manual-haul drill brings its axle to the exact coupling location; a crewman connects it before boarding. The ground-plane trailer keeps a fixed XY drawbar and stages swept weapon/obstacle tests through each accepted physical segment. Final rejected trailer rotation rolls back the tractor tick before gameplay effects. It is not a separately suspended 3D trailer rigid body. Tractor hull physics remains unchanged; towing pace is a provisional load heuristic, and configured mass limits are gameplay estimates, not certified historical ratings. PaK40 exceeds these light-carrier limits. Gun crew without a seat follow on foot; mortar capacity is sufficient for every living crewman.

Driver loss, interrupted loading, severe panic, total crew loss and missing vehicles terminate or unload without permanently reserving drivers or moving cargo to another carrier. Reservations, passenger/hatch state, cargo, tow pose/progress and pending orders are covered by the state hash. The legacy boarding-progress WeakMap was replaced by canonical state.

Animations show seated drivers/passengers (Kettenkrad rear bench faces backwards), hull-aware entry/exit, packed mortar tube/baseplate/bipod/ammunition lifting, crew coupling actions, closed trails, articulated drawbar and rolling wheel spokes. The Jeep has its own matte procedural mesh, four articulated wheels and a 2D fallback. Rendering never writes simulation state.
